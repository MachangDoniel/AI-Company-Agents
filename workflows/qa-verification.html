Verify completed implementation against acceptance criteria and regression risk.

PassMeaning
PassRequired behavior verified and no blocking regressions found.
FailA required behavior is broken.
BlockedQA cannot verify because environment, build, data, or access is missing.

Required Evidence

  • Steps run.
  • Expected and actual behavior.
  • Regression areas checked.
